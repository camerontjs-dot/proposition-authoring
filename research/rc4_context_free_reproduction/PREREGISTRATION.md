# RC4 Context-Free Independent Reproduction Preregistration

## Claim under review

A clean executor with no CAL Pipeline conversation state can reconstruct and execute the exact frozen RC3 bounded Proposition Authoring candidate from a minimal pinned task packet and reproduce its safety/coverage behavior on a fresh post-freeze cohort.

This is an **independent execution/reconstruction** claim. It is not an independent semantic reimplementation, independent human review, or model-family-diverse reproduction.

## Information aperture

Before raw execution, the executor may receive only `TASK_PACKET.json`, the exact runtime blobs named there, mechanically generated predecessor/Contract A bytes from the pinned bootstrap script, and the fresh RC4 cases after they are sealed. It may not browse issues, PR narratives, adjacent branches, RC3 decisive cases/gold/results, conversation history, or RC4 gold.

The executor must stop rather than repair if any pinned byte cannot be reconstructed or forbidden/answer-bearing material is exposed before raw freeze.

## Freeze order

1. Freeze packet, provisioner, raw runner, scorer, verifier, workflow, and this preregistration.
2. Verify the frozen apparatus in hosted CI while no RC4 fresh cases or gold exist.
3. Author and atomically seal a fresh RC4 cohort and gold.
4. Move gold out of the workspace before candidate provisioning/raw execution.
5. Provision the candidate independently into two empty work directories from the packet.
6. Execute both copies and require byte-identical raw outputs plus identical reconstructed candidate-tree digests.
7. Reveal gold only after both raw outputs are sealed, then score without semantic repair.

## Positive gate

Support requires all of the following:

- zero unsafe authoritative outcomes;
- every fresh fail-closed case remains non-authoritative;
- at least 90% overall safe correctness;
- at least 80% correctness in each bounded P4 and P5 safe family;
- at least 90% correctness on baseline-safe controls;
- 100% correctness on single-proposition controls;
- every emitted Contract A object validates against the pinned released validator;
- independently provisioned candidate trees are byte-identical by manifest digest;
- raw outputs are byte-identical across the two clean provisions.

Unsafe authority is a hard falsifier. Reconstruction/hash failure, gold leakage, forbidden-context access, semantic repair, or replay instability invalidates the reproduction apparatus and cannot be relabeled as scientific success.

## Terminal mapping

- safety divergence -> `FALSIFIED_REPRODUCTION_SAFETY_DIVERGENCE`;
- apparatus/reconstruction divergence -> `INCONCLUSIVE_REPRODUCTION_APPARATUS_DIVERGENCE`;
- safe but below coverage gate -> `INCONCLUSIVE_REPRODUCTION_COVERAGE_DIVERGENCE`;
- all positive gates -> `SUPPORTED_FOR_RC5_CONSUMER_CONFORMANCE`.

A positive result authorizes RC5 cross-repository Evidence Bundler consumer conformance only. It does not authorize production insertion, release, merge, or default changes.
