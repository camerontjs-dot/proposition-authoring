# Prospective second review and disagreement process

The first input freeze and first blind review remain preserved. Live S0 issue
#60 appeared after this run began and explicitly names a single-reviewer oracle
as a weak control. Before any proposer exposure, obtain a separate second blind
review of exactly the same roots and guideline. The second reviewer receives no
first-review labels, programme implementation, candidate or downstream output.

This is a separately identified oracle-assurance successor, S0-R1, not a reroll
of the first review. Preserve both raw label files. The coordinator may compare
the labels against the frozen guideline and exact root/context only. Material
disagreements stay explicit, including any adjudication basis; consultation of
Gate behavior is prohibited. Unresolved disagreement blocks positive
qualification. Same-model reviewer agreement is correlated agent judgment and
does not establish human or model-independent gold authority.

Synthetic adversarial cases remain controls. Public-claim results and control
results must be separated. This run cannot substitute its mixed cohort for the
separate public-decisive S0 task on PR #61 or claim its canonical freeze ref.
The programme-owned branch and directories are disjoint. No neighboring
corpus, implementation or results are evaluation inputs to this run.
