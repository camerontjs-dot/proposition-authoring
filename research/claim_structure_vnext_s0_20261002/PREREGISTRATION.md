# S0: freeze a discriminating evaluation problem

Question: can a targeted capability cohort and hostile suite distinguish
discovery failure, unsafe semantics, genuine ambiguity and unsupported
operators without selecting cases from Gate output?

Heaviest assumption: a blind qualitative reviewer can provide a usable bounded
semantic oracle. Review is agent judgment, not a deterministic or human oracle.
Falsifier: missing, forced, unresolved or apparatus-exposed annotation prevents
positive qualification. Preserve uncertain gold and continue only experiments
whose discrimination is independent of that uncertainty.

Subject: 24 exact roots, 12 pre-existing public NASA claims and 12 explicitly
synthetic adversarial/minimal-pair controls. No control supports prevalence.
Selection predates Gate execution. No RC0/RC1 case or review is reused.

Controls: atomic versus comparison; explicit/unmarked matrix attribution;
leading/trailing modifiers; roles and reference; quantifier scope; disjunction,
conditional and composite negation; harmless paraphrase and hostile mutations.
The mutation suite has independently constructed explicit expected validity.

Evaluator: one separate no-development-history agent review of the bounded
guideline and roots. The coordinator records the actual review identity and
limitations. No output from a candidate-generation arm is supplied. A reviewer
may not replace the selected cohort or inspect the implementation.

Stopping rule: seal every input, guideline, selector, review, mutation and
manifest before S1. All IDs remain in the denominator. Unresolved review stays
visible and prevents a supported programme result; it is not silently excluded.
Allowed dispositions: FROZEN_WORKING_ORACLE, INCONCLUSIVE_REVIEW, FAILED.

The corpus/gold are protected from runtime arms by process inputs. That is
documented aperture discipline, not an OS access-control guarantee. Exact
review traces remain local; public gold preserves semantic judgments and their
agent_llm provenance. Every later stage has its own preregistration and freeze.
