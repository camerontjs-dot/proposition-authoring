## Annotation rule

Reviewers receive only:

- exact root bytes;
- explicitly authorized context;
- source/provenance identity;
- this stage's annotation guide.

Reviewers must not receive:

- ClaimGate output;
- proposer output;
- model rationale/confidence;
- Evidence Bundler/CAL/Decision output;
- downstream retrieval or verifier performance.

Each reviewer first records an independent raw label.

Allowed adjudication outcomes:

- ATOMIC
- ALL_OF
- ANY_OF
- IF_THEN
- SCOPED
- OTHER
- AMBIGUOUS
- EXCLUDE_INSUFFICIENT_ADJUDICATION

`COVERAGE_LIMITATION`, `REPRESENTATION_UNSUPPORTED`, and `FAILED` are runtime/research terminal observations, not labels to force onto the semantic gold before the system under test runs.

Material disagreements remain inspectable and are never overwritten.

## Gold sufficiency gate

S0 may freeze only after:

- source identity and exact case bytes are content-bound;
- reviewer-specific raw labels exist;
- material disagreements are preserved;
- adjudication uses the annotation rule rather than current V1 behavior;
- every target family has enough cases to exercise the intended distinction;
- no successor proposer/verifier output has been exposed to corpus selection or gold.

If independent review cannot be obtained, terminal disposition is:

`BLOCKED_INSUFFICIENT_REVIEW_AUTHORITY`

Do not manufacture gold.
