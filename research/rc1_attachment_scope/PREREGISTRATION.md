## Research question

Can a bounded root-level ambiguity gate preserve materially distinct attachment/scope interpretations before a candidate decomposition acquires authority, while leaving clear counterparts resolvable under the unchanged V0 proposer and RC1 semantic evaluator?

## Hypothesis

A small typed root-interpretation layer can detect specific cases where a scope-bearing construction admits at least two materially different clause-attachment analyses. If such a finding remains unresolved, the apparatus should `ABSTAINED` before Contract A emission even when the existing candidate/evaluator path leaves one surviving semantic cluster.

The gate is not a parser and is not decomposition authority. It is a promotion-blocking semantic hazard measurement.

## Target scope

RC1 may change only:

- root-level attachment/scope ambiguity detection;
- the engine rule that prevents authority when such a finding exists;
- receipt instrumentation needed to preserve the finding;
- RC1-specific tests/harness/records.

The frozen P1/P2/P3 proposal logic and frozen RC1 candidate evaluator remain unchanged. No reranker, LLM judge, retrieval, CAL, Decision Engine, or downstream result may participate.

## Bounded ambiguity families

The target may recognize only preregistered structural families:

1. matrix attribution/evidential predicate over coordinated clause-like material where the matrix scope may end before or after the coordination;
2. trailing adjunct attachment where a final temporal/location/condition-like modifier may attach locally to the second conjunct or to the coordinated proposition as a whole;
3. explicit sentential-negation constructions where negation scope over a coordinated proposition remains structurally unresolved.

Explicit disambiguators may include repeated matrix predicates, repeated complementizers, correlative `both`, repeated local adjuncts, or structurally explicit equivalents. Punctuation alone is not sufficient authority.

## Development-only cases

Already observed evidence may be used for development:

- V0 Q29 unsafe attribution-scope emission;
- predecessor F20 local-attribution parser failure;
- V0 Q21 clear explicit-`that` attribution counterpart;
- V0/Q predecessor single-proposition controls;
- existing explicit shared/local operator examples.

These cases cannot count as fresh decisive evidence.

## Weak controls

At minimum compare conceptually or executable where practical:

- C0: unchanged V0, no ambiguity gate;
- C1: blanket reporting-verb + conjunction => abstain;
- C2: punctuation-only ambiguity detector;
- C3: first surviving interpretation wins;
- C4: majority/agreement count grants authority.

The target should outperform weak controls by preserving clear counterparts while failing closed on genuine ambiguity.

## Freeze order

1. implement and test target only on development/regression evidence;
2. freeze target source, ambiguity profile, engine rule, scorer, controls, and meta tests by Git identity;
3. only after freeze, author the fresh cases and gold;
4. freeze fresh cases/gold separately before target execution;
5. run raw target outputs without gold in runtime reach;
6. require exact replay;
7. reveal gold only for scoring.

Any post-result semantic target repair requires a successor experiment.

## Fresh decisive surface

After target freeze, create at least 24 fresh roots with minimal-pair structure across:

- ambiguous unmarked attribution scope;
- clear repeated attribution predicate;
- clear repeated complementizer / `both` forms;
- ambiguous trailing temporal/location adjunct scope;
- clear prefix-shared and repeated-local adjunct counterparts;
- ambiguous sentential negation over coordination;
- clear repeated/local negation counterparts;
- punctuation variants that must not change material interpretation by themselves;
- simple single-proposition controls.

Fresh gold must specify the required authoring disposition and, for clear decomposition cases, permissible child semantics. Internal adjudication limitations must be preserved.

## Hard falsifiers

RC1 is `FALSIFIED` if any of the following occurs on the frozen fresh surface:

- any preregistered materially ambiguous root emits authoritative `DECLARED` or `NOT_NEEDED`;
- a clear counterpart is converted into a materially wrong authoritative decomposition;
- candidate/proposer ordering changes final authority;
- exact replay changes;
- fresh gold becomes visible to runtime authority before raw output freeze;
- downstream evidence/outcomes influence ambiguity resolution;
- target semantics are repaired after fresh output is observed.

## Positive burden

A positive RC1 requires all of:

- zero unsafe authoritative outcomes on fresh ambiguity cases;
- all preregistered clear attribution counterparts resolved correctly;
- all simple single-proposition controls remain correct;
- no material regression on the development V0 safe cases used as regression only;
- exact replay;
- metamorphic invariance for punctuation/spacing/candidate ordering where meaning is unchanged;
- weak controls fail discriminating cases;
- hosted execution evidence if available.

Coverage outside this bounded ambiguity profile is not required for RC1 support and may remain `ABSTAINED`.
