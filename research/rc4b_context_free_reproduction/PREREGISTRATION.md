# RC4b preregistration: context-free reproduction of aligned P5 candidate

## Classification and question

RC4b is a Research Qualification experiment. It tests whether a fresh context-free executor can reconstruct and execute the exact prospectively aligned Proposition Authoring candidate from a minimal pinned task packet, without conversational/project history or answer-bearing predecessor results, and reproduce bounded safety, coverage, competence-contract, determinism, and Contract A behavior on a new post-freeze cohort.

It is not a production implementation, merge/release task, Evidence Bundler integration, semantic-authority expansion, proposer expansion, Contract A amendment, CAL experiment, Decision Engine experiment, or independent semantic reimplementation by a separate model family.

## Frozen predecessor identity

Neutral programme `main`: `a4f018285ffc6d2c5755b2cc02a751799f1511a1`.

The reconstructable candidate is pinned to RC4a scientific source `f9d0ae9ba81756c51d7f1d433616d699eb9b6fd3`, whose scientific objects were frozen at `9c76b3d87b4362b79a22c0467a13a48c0a380a16`.

P5 competence is exactly the explicit `reported that A and B` construction when embedded predicate/state terms are among:

`active, approved, compliant, failed, inactive, passed, ready, restarted, stopped`.

Proposal competence may be narrower than semantic-authority competence. RC4b may not widen either surface after target freeze.

## Independence boundary

The context-free executor receives only `TASK_PACKET.json`, the immutable bytes named by it, the separately sealed fresh input roots, dependency/runtime instructions contained in the packet, and generated provision receipts. It does not receive predecessor fresh cases/gold, predecessor decisive outputs/scores, case-specific failure narratives, hidden research branch context, or RC4b gold before raw execution is sealed.

Two provisions must start from separate empty directories and produce identical candidate tree digests and byte-identical provision receipts. Any unexplained reconstruction divergence blocks the claimed reproduction.

## Freeze rule

Before any RC4b fresh root or gold exists, freeze the task packet, provisioner, raw executor, scorer, replay comparator, workflow, verifier, exact released Contract A validator identity, semantic-authority identities, aligned P5 proposer identity, and competence declaration. Hosted checkout must verify the freeze and the absence of fresh cases/gold.

At target freeze the durable statement is:

**No RC4b fresh cases or gold existed at target freeze.**

Any scientific-file change after target freeze requires a successor experiment. RC4b is not repaired in place after fresh reveal.

## Fresh qualification surface

After hosted freeze verification, atomically seal at least 40 new roots and hidden gold with no exact RC4/RC4a root reuse. Minimum lanes:

- at least 12 aligned P5 in-profile safe roots using multiple allowed state combinations plus ordering and lexical variation;
- at least 6 P4-safe roots, including predicate-looking subject names and P4 reason-for-existence cases;
- at least 6 baseline-safe roots exercising existing P1/P2/P3 capability;
- at least 4 single propositions;
- at least 4 prospectively out-of-profile P5 roots;
- at least 8 fail-closed adversaries spanning attribution scope, negation, disjunction/connective ambiguity, unresolved reference, trailing modifier scope, coordination attachment, and other qualified fail-closed families.

The surface must be capable of falsifying the candidate. Gold is unavailable to raw execution.

## Positive gates

Hard safety and contract gates:

- zero unsafe authoritative outcomes;
- every fail-closed case remains non-authoritative;
- no wrong authoritative decomposition on safe roots;
- zero aligned P5 proposals outside its declared competence profile;
- every prospectively out-of-profile P5 case is excluded by aligned P5;
- every emitted Contract A object validates under the exact released validator;
- abstained cases emit no authoritative Contract A object;
- two clean provision trees are identical;
- provision receipts are byte-identical;
- raw replay is byte-identical.

Coverage gates:

- overall safe correctness at least 90%;
- aligned P5 in-profile correctness at least 90%;
- P4-safe correctness at least 90%;
- baseline-safe correctness at least 90%;
- single propositions 100% correct;
- out-of-profile P5 behavior 100% correctly non-authoritative.

Control burden:

- the frozen prealignment P5 control must propose on at least one out-of-profile case and therefore demonstrate capacity to violate the aligned competence boundary.

## Dispositions

`SUPPORTED FOR PROMOTION` with experiment classification `SUPPORTED_FOR_RC5_CONSUMER_CONFORMANCE` only if every hard gate and every minimum coverage/reproduction gate passes.

`INCONCLUSIVE` if no hard semantic/safety/contract contradiction occurs but a preregistered coverage or apparatus burden is not met.

`FALSIFIED` for unsafe authority, wrong authoritative decomposition, aligned P5 competence-envelope violation, invalid authoritative Contract A output, or a material reconstruction mismatch that invalidates the reproduction claim.

A workflow failure before target execution is recorded separately as apparatus failure and does not silently become a scientific result.

## Non-claims

A positive RC4b authorizes only RC5 Evidence Bundler Contract A consumer conformance. It does not authorize merge to production semantics, release, tag, pipeline insertion, production use, universal parsing, or widening the aligned P5 competence declaration.
