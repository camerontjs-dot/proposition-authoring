# Stopping rule

Stop when one of these is true:

1. One of the four admission dispositions is justified by the frozen validator, the eligible reviews, and the hostile-copy results.
2. A certificate or protocol defect appears after the decisive freeze. Record it. Do not repair the frozen objects. A smaller successor needs a new identity.
3. No eligible reviewer profile remains among the frozen profiles.
4. The next step would edit frozen V1, Contract A, product responsibility, or a production dependency.

Do not start S1 proposal discovery from this packet unless the disposition is `SUPPORTED_CERTIFICATE_SURFACE_FOR_S0_PACKET`. That support still does not authorize S1 by itself. It authorizes preparation of a fresh targeted S0 packet only.
