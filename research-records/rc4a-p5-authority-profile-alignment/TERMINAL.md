## Interpretation

RC4a supports the narrower architectural rule that a proposal lane should declare and enforce its own competence envelope prospectively, rather than emitting candidates beyond the authority profile and depending on downstream rejection to clean them up.

The positive result does **not** establish that `rejected` is semantically undecomposable. It establishes only that `rejected` is outside this bounded P5 + frozen-authority profile. A richer successor may expand that semantic surface only through a separately qualified authority change.

The result also supports keeping proposer competence narrower than semantic authority when useful. Authority capability is not, by itself, permission for a proposer to claim a broader surface.
