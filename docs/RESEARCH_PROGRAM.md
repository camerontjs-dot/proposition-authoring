# Proposition Authoring Research Programme

## Programme goal

Establish a bounded Proposition Authoring apparatus that can convert an exact authoritative root proposition into either a warranted Contract A 2.0.0 declaration or an explicit fail-closed non-authoritative outcome, without using downstream retrieval, CAL, Decision Engine, or consumer performance as semantic authority.

The programme is successful only when semantic safety, useful bounded coverage, deterministic provenance, evaluator assurance, exact contract conformance, and independent reproduction are all supported together.

## Current evidence state

### Supported with bounds

- A separate Proposition Authoring ownership boundary is justified.
- Plural proposal is more promising than relying on one sovereign decomposer.
- Vote count, proposer popularity, or first-proposal selection cannot grant authority.
- Contract A can carry exact root/decomposition lineage, but Contract A validity does not prove decomposition semantics.
- Conservative abstention is preferable to silently resolving material ambiguity.

### Falsified

Frozen V0 is falsified for promotion because Q29 acquired authoritative output despite a materially live attribution-scope alternative.

### Important unresolved coverage failures

V0 also preserved three safe false negatives:

- Q09: proposal coverage gap on a safe shared-subject root;
- Q18: a safe local-negation candidate was rejected by the frozen authority layer;
- Q21: a shared-attribution case with explicit `that` remained unresolved because the available candidate lost attribution scope and was correctly rejected.

These are not reasons to relax the safety boundary. They are later coverage/authority research targets.

## Research strategy

Run the programme serially. Do not combine safety repair, proposer expansion, evaluator replacement, and cross-repository integration in one experiment. Each stage should answer one discriminating question and freeze before fresh decisive evidence.

The ordering is intentional:

```text
semantic safety boundary
    -> authority/evaluator assurance
    -> safe coverage recovery
    -> independent reproduction
    -> cross-repository consumer conformance
    -> smallest justified promotion candidate
```

A later stage does not repair a failed earlier stage. It starts a successor experiment with new frozen identities.

## Experiment 1 - Attachment and scope ambiguity authority gate

### Research question

Can the apparatus detect when more than one materially distinct attachment/scope interpretation remains live and prevent any one decomposition from acquiring authority, while still resolving clear attribution/coordination counterparts?

### Why this is next

Q29 is a direct hard-safety falsifier. The smallest missing capability is not more proposal volume. It is preserving competing root interpretations before resolution.

### Preferred architecture under test

Treat root interpretation itself as a hypothesis surface. A candidate decomposition is authority-eligible only if it is semantically conservative across the admissible root interpretation set, or if the root interpretation set collapses uniquely under explicit bounded evidence.

Do not implement a blanket lexical rule such as `reported + and => abstain` as the target architecture. Such a rule is acceptable only as a weak control.

### Development/regression surface

Use already-observed cases only for development:

- Q29 unsafe attribution ambiguity;
- predecessor F20 greedy attribution parse;
- Q21 explicit `that` as a clear attribution-preserving counterpart;
- several existing clear explicit-clause and shared-subject cases;
- V0 `NOT_NEEDED` controls to ensure the new gate does not become a global abstention switch.

### Fresh decisive surface

Construct minimal-pair families after target/gates freeze. Include at least:

- unmarked reporting scope versus explicit `that` / repeated reporting predicate;
- matrix predicate versus embedded coordination;
- local versus shared temporal/location modifiers;
- local negation versus shared negation;
- punctuation variants that must not become authority by themselves;
- explicit disambiguators such as `both`, repeated subjects, repeated matrix predicates, or structurally equivalent clear forms.

### Weak controls

- V0 unchanged;
- blanket `reporting verb + conjunction => abstain`;
- punctuation-only ambiguity detector;
- majority-vote interpretation selection;
- first-generated interpretation selection.

### Hard falsifiers

- any preregistered materially ambiguous fresh root emits authoritative `DECLARED` or `NOT_NEEDED`;
- candidate or proposer ordering changes the authority outcome;
- the gate consults downstream outcomes;
- fresh gold or expected disposition becomes visible to runtime authority;
- post-result semantic repair of the frozen target.

### Positive burden

- zero unsafe authoritative emissions;
- clear minimal-pair counterparts remain resolvable at the preregistered level;
- V0 single-proposition controls remain correct;
- exact replay;
- weak controls fail discriminating cases.

### Authorization if supported

Authorize a successor integrated candidate with the ambiguity mechanism frozen. Do not authorize production insertion.

## Experiment 2 - Independent authority instruments and disagreement semantics

### Research question

Once attachment ambiguity is represented explicitly, can more than one independent semantic authority instrument reduce single-evaluator blind spots without turning agreement or majority vote into truth?

### Key assumption under test

The frozen RC1 authority layer is itself a system under test. It rejected safe Q18 and accepted unsafe Q29. A safer architecture may need independent measurements rather than one semantic parser acting as final authority.

### Candidate design

Use at least two genuinely different authority instruments over a common proposition/binding representation. Examples may include:

- a deterministic binding/scope checker;
- a separately implemented structural/semantic checker;
- a tightly constrained LLM checklist reader used only as a measurement instrument, never as sovereign author.

The resolver should consume typed findings. It must not use majority vote. Critical unresolved disagreement should fail closed.

### Development surface

- Q18 safe local negation;
- Q29 unsafe attribution ambiguity;
- RC1 false-negative families around reference and local attribution;
- known unsafe binding/scope mutations from earlier evaluator work.

### Fresh decisive surface

Mutation and minimal-pair cases where one instrument is deliberately likely to miss the defect. Include adversarial evaluator controls so a panel that merely agrees on easy cases cannot pass.

### Hard falsifiers

- majority vote can override a critical semantic objection;
- correlated instruments are presented as independent without evidence;
- a weak instrument passes the same promotion gate as the target;
- target safety depends on one unqualified measurement source;
- unsafe fresh authority appears.

### Authorization if supported

Freeze the authority-composition rule as a reusable research component. Do not yet optimize coverage.

## Experiment 3 - Safe coverage recovery

### Research question

With the safety and authority boundaries frozen, can proposal diversity recover known safe false negatives without increasing unsafe authority?

### Primary targets

- Q09 proposal-coverage gap;
- Q18 safe local-negation coverage once authority treatment is corrected;
- Q21 explicit shared-attribution coverage;
- other safe families that abstain because no valid candidate is generated.

### Candidate proposer lanes

Start small. Prefer one new structurally distinct proposal lane at a time, such as:

- clause/coordination structural proposer;
- binding/frame proposer;
- one constrained LLM semantic proposer with frozen model/version/prompt/settings where reproducible enough.

All outputs normalize into the same candidate schema and remain non-authoritative until the frozen safety/authority apparatus admits them.

### Controls

- original V0 proposers only;
- new proposer alone;
- proposer majority vote;
- unrestricted LLM 'best decomposition' judge.

### Hard falsifiers

- any recovered coverage is bought by new unsafe authority;
- proposer identity/count grants authority;
- downstream performance selects between decompositions;
- changing proposer order changes final authority.

### Positive burden

Pre-register a bounded safe-coverage improvement over the frozen predecessor while preserving zero unsafe authoritative outcomes and deterministic replay.

### Authorization if supported

Authorize a full bounded candidate for independent qualification.

## Experiment 4 - Independent reproduction and consumer conformance

### Research question

Can an independent/context-free implementer or consumer recover the frozen candidate's intended behavior from public specifications and artifacts, and can Evidence Bundler consume valid outputs without hidden coupling?

### Required evidence

- fresh independent reproduction from a constrained aperture;
- exact frozen specs/schema/receipts;
- no access to reference implementation before freeze where practical;
- Contract A producer validation;
- Evidence Bundler as an independent consumer of emitted Contract A objects;
- negative conformance cases for abstention, failure, altered hashes, reordered children, and malformed lineage.

### Hard falsifiers

- independent implementation requires undocumented repository knowledge;
- consumer behavior depends on proposer/evaluator internals rather than Contract A;
- invalid or semantically unauthorized outputs pass consumer conformance;
- reproduction changes frozen semantics to pass.

### Authorization if supported

Only then consider a minimal promotion proposal for the bounded capability actually reproduced and consumed.

## Promotion boundary

No proposition-authoring implementation should be proposed for pipeline insertion until all of the following are supported on exact frozen artifacts:

1. zero unsafe authoritative outcomes on preregistered fresh adversarial qualification for the promoted profile;
2. useful bounded safe coverage and correct `NOT_NEEDED` handling under explicit stage-specific gates;
3. deterministic replay and proposition identity invariants;
4. exact Contract A producer conformance;
5. promotion-critical evaluator/authority instrumentation has survived weak controls and adversarial tests;
6. at least one independent/context-free reproduction or equivalent costly-to-fake implementation test;
7. cross-repository consumer conformance with Evidence Bundler;
8. no downstream selection or semantic repair loop;
9. failures, abstentions, deviations, and residual unsupported families remain explicit.

Promotion, if reached, should be a new minimal PR from a clean production base. Research branches are evidence records, not production authorization.

## Programme stop / rethink triggers

Pause and reconsider the architecture rather than accumulating patches if any of these recur:

- each new semantic family requires a bespoke lexical exception;
- the ambiguity gate grows into an untested universal parser;
- independent authority instruments share the same hidden parser/failure mode;
- safe coverage improves only when fail-closed semantics are weakened;
- the apparatus requires CAL/retrieval/downstream outcomes to choose decomposition;
- repeated fresh cohorts expose materially new unsafe-authority families with no common abstraction.

Those patterns would suggest the current representation or authority boundary is wrong, not merely incomplete.
