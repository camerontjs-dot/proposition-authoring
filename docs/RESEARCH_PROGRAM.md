# Proposition Authoring Research Programme

## Programme goal

Establish a bounded Proposition Authoring apparatus that can convert one exact authoritative root proposition into either a warranted Contract A 2.0.0 declaration or an explicit fail-closed non-authoritative outcome, without using downstream retrieval, CAL, Decision Engine, or consumer performance as semantic authority.

The programme is successful only when semantic safety, useful bounded coverage, deterministic provenance, evaluator assurance, exact contract conformance, independent reproduction, and cross-repository consumer conformance are supported together.

The target is not a universal semantic parser. The target is explicit bounded competence with reproducible provenance and fail-closed behavior outside the qualified profile.

## Current evidence state

### Supported with bounds

- A separate Proposition Authoring ownership boundary is justified.
- Plural proposal is more promising than relying on one sovereign decomposer.
- Vote count, proposer popularity, reranker score, or first-proposal selection cannot grant authority.
- Contract A can carry exact root/decomposition lineage, but Contract A validity does not prove decomposition semantics.
- Conservative abstention is preferable to silently resolving material ambiguity.
- The first integrated V0 demonstrated deterministic replay, exact Contract A conformance for emitted handoffs, correct bounded `NOT_NEEDED` behavior on its fresh controls, and useful but incomplete safe coverage.

### Falsified

Frozen V0 is falsified for promotion because Q29 acquired authoritative output despite a materially live attribution-scope alternative.

### Important unresolved safe false negatives

- Q09: proposal coverage gap on a safe shared-subject root.
- Q18: a safe local-negation candidate was rejected by the frozen authority layer.
- Q21: a shared-attribution case with explicit `that` remained unresolved because the available candidate lost attribution scope and was correctly rejected.

These are not reasons to relax the safety boundary. They are later coverage/authority research targets.

## Research strategy

Run the programme serially. Do not combine safety repair, proposer expansion, evaluator replacement, independent reproduction, and cross-repository integration in one experiment. Each stage should answer one discriminating question and freeze before fresh decisive evidence.

```text
semantic safety boundary
    -> authority/evaluator assurance
    -> safe coverage recovery
    -> independent reproduction
    -> cross-repository consumer conformance
    -> naturalistic shadow qualification
    -> smallest justified promotion candidate
```

A later stage does not repair a failed earlier stage. It starts a successor experiment with new frozen identities.

## Programme invariants

Unless a dedicated architecture experiment explicitly targets one of these boundaries:

- retrieval, Evidence Bundler outputs, CAL conclusions, Decision Engine outcomes, and downstream success may not select or repair a decomposition;
- no majority-vote, proposer-count, reranker-score, or model-confidence rule may grant semantic authority;
- all candidate proposals, evaluations, rejections, ambiguity states, and final decisions must remain receipt-visible;
- `ABSTAINED` must not be silently coerced into Contract A authority;
- `FAILED` remains distinct from semantic abstention;
- `NOT_NEEDED` requires positive bounded evidence for one proposition and may not be inferred from proposer silence;
- exact Contract A output must validate against the released contract and bind exact root/child/source identity;
- fresh decisive cases are authored only after scientific objects and evaluator policy are frozen;
- failures and false negatives are preserved rather than repaired inside a frozen experiment.

## RC1 - Attachment and scope ambiguity authority gate

Canonical issue: #5.

### Research question

Can the apparatus detect when more than one materially distinct attachment/scope interpretation remains live and prevent any one decomposition from acquiring authority, while still resolving clear attribution/coordination counterparts?

### Why this is first

Q29 is a direct hard-safety falsifier. The smallest missing capability is not more proposal volume. It is preserving competing root interpretations before resolution.

### Preferred architecture under test

Treat root interpretation itself as a hypothesis surface. A candidate decomposition is authority-eligible only if it is semantically conservative across the admissible root interpretation set, or if the root interpretation set collapses uniquely under explicit bounded evidence.

Do not make the target a lexical patch such as `reported + and => abstain`. That is a weak control, not the architecture.

### Development-only predecessor cases

- V0 Q29 unsafe attribution ambiguity;
- predecessor F20 greedy attribution parse;
- V0 Q21 explicit `that` as a clear attribution-preserving counterpart;
- clear explicit-clause/shared-subject cases;
- V0 `NOT_NEEDED` controls.

### Fresh decisive families

- unmarked reporting scope vs explicit `that` / repeated reporting predicate;
- matrix predicate vs embedded coordination;
- local vs shared temporal/location modifiers;
- local vs shared negation/modality where attachment is genuinely ambiguous;
- punctuation variants that must not create authority;
- explicit disambiguators such as `both`, repeated subjects, or repeated matrix predicates;
- paraphrase and child-order metamorphic variants.

### Weak controls

- V0 unchanged;
- blanket reporting-verb/conjunction abstention;
- punctuation-only ambiguity detector;
- majority-vote interpretation selection;
- first-generated interpretation selection.

### Hard falsifiers

- any preregistered materially ambiguous fresh root emits authoritative `DECLARED` or `NOT_NEEDED`;
- candidate/proposer ordering changes authority outcome;
- runtime authority sees fresh gold or expected disposition;
- downstream outcomes affect selection;
- post-result semantic repair of the frozen target.

### Positive burden

- zero unsafe authoritative emissions;
- clear minimal-pair counterparts remain resolvable at preregistered coverage;
- V0 single-proposition controls remain correct;
- exact replay;
- weak controls fail discriminating cases.

A positive RC1 authorizes only RC2 composition work.

## RC2 - Independent semantic instruments and disagreement semantics

Canonical issue: #6. Run only after RC1 reaches a terminal result that justifies continuation.

### Research question

Once attachment ambiguity is represented explicitly, can more than one genuinely independent semantic instrument reduce single-evaluator blind spots without turning agreement into truth?

### Key assumption under test

The frozen RC1-style authority layer is itself a system under test. It rejected safe Q18 and accepted unsafe Q29. A safer architecture may need independent typed measurements rather than one semantic parser acting as final authority.

### Candidate lanes

At least two materially different implementation paths, for example:

- deterministic binding/scope checker;
- separately implemented structural/semantic checker;
- tightly constrained LLM checklist reader used only as measurement;
- alternative deterministic relation/frame representation.

Two wrappers around the same parser/model do not count as independent.

### Resolver semantics

Preregister explicitly. Default research policy:

- deterministic falsifier or unresolved critical ambiguity blocks authority;
- semantically equivalent acceptable analyses may converge;
- materially distinct surviving analyses => `ABSTAINED`;
- unresolved critical instrument disagreement => `ABSTAINED`;
- instrument count never decides correctness.

### Fresh burden

Use adversarial cross-instrument cases where one instrument is likely to miss the defect. Include mutations that can make one lane appear confident while another exposes binding, scope, operator, reference, or propositionhood loss.

### Hard falsifiers

- majority vote overrides a critical objection;
- correlated instruments are represented as independent;
- weak controls pass the same gate as the target;
- target safety depends on one unqualified measurement source;
- any unsafe fresh authority appears.

A positive RC2 freezes an authority-composition rule for RC3. It does not optimize coverage yet.

## RC3 - Safe coverage recovery under frozen safety boundary

Canonical issue: #7. Run only after RC1 and RC2 justify continuation.

### Research question

With safety and authority rules frozen, can proposal diversity recover known safe false negatives without increasing unsafe authority?

### Primary development targets

- Q09 proposal-coverage gap;
- Q18 safe local-negation coverage once authority treatment is corrected;
- Q21 explicit shared-attribution coverage;
- later safe abstentions accumulated through RC1/RC2.

### Method

Add only one new proposal capability at a time. Compare:

- frozen baseline proposer set;
- each new proposer alone;
- pooled proposals under exactly the same frozen authority/resolution rules.

Candidate order, proposer order, duplicate votes, and harmless surface variation must not change the authoritative result.

### Candidate proposer lanes

Prefer structurally distinct lanes, such as:

- coordination/clause structural proposer;
- binding/frame proposer;
- one constrained LLM semantic proposer with frozen model/version/prompt/settings where reproducible enough.

All outputs normalize into the same candidate schema and remain non-authoritative until admitted by the frozen safety/authority apparatus.

### Positive burden

Pre-register a bounded safe-coverage improvement over the frozen predecessor across more than one semantic family, with zero additional unsafe authoritative outcomes and exact replay.

A positive RC3 authorizes one integrated bounded candidate for independent qualification, not production insertion.

## RC4 - Independent/context-free reproduction

Run only after RC1-RC3 support one exact integrated candidate.

### Research question

Can an independent executor reproduce the frozen candidate and its qualification result using only a frozen task packet and pinned public authorities?

### Required evidence

- exact source/tree/model/prompt/config identities;
- context-free execution packet with no hidden conversation assumptions;
- fresh decisive cohort not used during development;
- frozen evaluator/gold lineage;
- independent execution or implementation path where practical;
- exact receipt/checksum comparison;
- deviations preserved rather than normalized away.

### Hard falsifiers

- one unsafe authoritative outcome;
- material semantic divergence from the frozen reference;
- task requires undocumented repository/conversation knowledge;
- reproduction alters semantics to obtain a pass;
- evaluator outcome cannot be independently reconstructed.

A positive RC4 authorizes consumer-conformance testing only.

## RC5 - Cross-repository Contract A consumer conformance

Run only after RC4 supports the candidate.

### Research question

Can Evidence Bundler consume Proposition Authoring outputs strictly through released Contract A without hidden coupling or semantic rewriting?

### Required evidence

Use frozen Proposition Authoring outputs and an independent Evidence Bundler consumer path. Verify:

- exact Contract A 2.0.0 validation;
- child identity and lineage preservation;
- `not_decomposed` behavior;
- no handoff on `ABSTAINED`;
- explicit handling of processing failure;
- no Evidence Bundler rewrite of authoritative proposition text/identity;
- negative cases for altered hashes, malformed lineage, missing children, unexpected ordering assumptions, and unsupported decomposition state;
- cross-repository fixture hashes and exact replay.

This is consumer conformance, not retrieval-quality evaluation.

### Hard falsifiers

- consumer requires proposer/evaluator internals rather than Contract A;
- consumer rewrites authoritative propositions;
- invalid/unauthorized handoffs are silently accepted;
- conformance depends on hidden same-repo fixtures or undocumented assumptions.

A positive RC5 authorizes shadow naturalistic qualification only.

## RC6 - Bounded naturalistic shadow qualification

Run only after RC4 and RC5 support the candidate.

### Research question

Does the qualified bounded profile survive a small frozen cohort of realistic claims representative of intended CAL Pipeline usage without exposing a new unsafe-authority family?

### Method

- freeze a small naturalistic cohort before execution;
- keep all outputs shadow-only;
- preserve source/context bytes and provenance;
- classify coverage, abstention reasons, failure families, and Contract A conformance;
- do not use Evidence Bundler/CAL outcomes to repair authoring.

### Promotion-blocking observations

- any new unsafe authoritative decomposition;
- profile boundary materially fails to predict observed competence;
- abstention reasons collapse into opaque catch-alls;
- new unrelated failure families suggest the representation is structurally incomplete.

If supported, RC6 may authorize drafting the smallest possible promotion proposal. It does not itself merge or release anything.

## Promotion boundary

No proposition-authoring implementation should be proposed for pipeline insertion until all applicable gates are supported on exact frozen artifacts:

1. zero unsafe authoritative outcomes on final preregistered fresh adversarial qualification for the promoted profile;
2. useful bounded safe coverage and correct `NOT_NEEDED` handling under explicit stage-specific gates;
3. deterministic replay and proposition identity invariants;
4. exact released Contract A producer conformance;
5. promotion-critical evaluator/authority instrumentation survives weak controls, mutation/metamorphic tests, and adversarial cases;
6. at least one independent/context-free reproduction or equivalent costly-to-fake implementation test;
7. cross-repository Evidence Bundler consumer conformance;
8. bounded naturalistic shadow execution exposes no new promotion-blocking unsafe family;
9. no downstream semantic-selection or repair loop;
10. failures, abstentions, deviations, and residual unsupported families remain explicit.

Promotion, if reached, must be a new minimal PR from a clean production base and include only the capability supported by those gates. Research PR success is not production authorization.

## Programme stop / rethink triggers

Pause and reconsider the architecture rather than accumulating patches if any recur:

- each new semantic family requires a bespoke lexical exception;
- the ambiguity gate grows into an untested universal parser;
- independent authority instruments share the same hidden parser/model failure mode;
- safe coverage improves only when fail-closed semantics are weakened;
- the apparatus requires retrieval/CAL/Decision outcomes to choose decomposition;
- independent reproduction repeatedly fails because of hidden implementation context;
- repeated fresh cohorts expose materially new unsafe-authority families with no common abstraction;
- the qualified profile becomes impossible to describe compactly and prospectively.

Those patterns would suggest the current representation or authority boundary is wrong, not merely incomplete.

## Long-term goal

The goal is not maximal automatic decomposition. The goal is a small, auditable upstream authoring apparatus whose emitted proposition authority is trustworthy enough that downstream Evidence Bundler and CAL can treat the Contract A proposition set as genuinely authorized semantic structure rather than an opaque heuristic rewrite.
